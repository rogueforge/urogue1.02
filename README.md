UltraRogue is a dungeon game in the line of Rogue, written by Herb
Chong. You choose one of four character classes, fighter, magician,
cleric or thief, and go down through a hundred levels of rooms, mazes
and throne rooms, past trading posts, quartermasters and monsters that
summon others, to bring back the Amulet of Yendor and the other
artifacts.

This is UltraRogue 1.02, dated August 13, 1985.

What is different from 1.02 Beta of May 19, 1985:

- A wand of blasting, and grenades that blow holes in the floor.
- Rings of vampiric regeneration, and rings of carrying that come in
  degrees; the ring of burden is gone.
- Resurrection depends on constitution, luck and the ring, and owned
  and protected things stay with a hero brought back.
- Trading posts price their goods when they open, remember what the
  hero sold them, and give a hero wearing a ring of adornment four more
  transactions. Anything can be claimed with a scroll of ownership.
- Throne rooms are less deadly; leprechauns and nymphs are tougher, and
  nymphs curse what they look through.
- Gazes do not work on an invisible hero, and super heroism ends fear.
- Job control on 4.x BSD, and support for System III.
